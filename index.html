Your data stays in your browser unless you choose to share it

This site saves your salary history in your browser using local storage on this device. It is not sent to any server. The only way data leaves this site is by using the share button. If you want to share a copy, you can choose a share link that keeps your real salary values, or a share link that hides them.

The point of this site

This is a simple way to see how your pay changes over time. It compares your salary growth against inflation so you can tell whether your money is really going up in real life, not just on paper.
